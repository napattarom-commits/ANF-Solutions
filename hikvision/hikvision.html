About HIKVISION

HIKVISION is a leading global provider of security and IoT solutions. Its fire safety business unit applies decades of perception and AI know-how to fire alarm panels, intelligent detectors, and vision-based flame detection cameras — protecting commercial, industrial, and residential properties worldwide.

In Thailand, ANF Solutions is a retailer of HIKVISION fire safety products, providing local stock, system design support, installation services, and after-sales coordination.

Why HIKVISION fire safety?

End-to-end portfolio

Conventional, addressable, LoRa wireless, vision-based, and standalone — one brand, one ecosystem.

AI-enabled perception

Vision-based flame and heat detection with proven AI algorithms minimise false alarms.

Global standards

Designed to internationally recognised fire safety standards with rigorous manufacturer QA.

Local support

Fully supported in Thailand — local stock, sales engineering, installation, spare parts, and training.

AI fire detection technology

HIKVISION's fusion fire detection cameras combine three sensing modalities — thermal imaging, dual-infrared flame sensing, and visible-light video — applying AI analytics to deliver early, accurate, and reliable fire detection in large open spaces such as warehouses, EV battery storage, petrochemical sites, tunnels, and logistics centres.

  • Detect fire at the source — earlier than traditional ceiling-mounted detectors
  • Sub-second response and on-camera AI verification
  • Integration with VMS, BMS, and fire alarm control panels
  • Wide coverage with a single sensor — lower install cost for large areas
