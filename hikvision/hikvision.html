AI Fire Detection Technology

HIKVISION's fusion fire detection cameras combine three sensing modalities — thermal imaging, dual-infrared flame sensing and visible-light video — and apply AI analytics to deliver early, accurate and reliable fire detection in large open spaces and high-risk environments such as warehouses, EV battery storage, petrochemical sites, tunnels and logistics centers.

  • Detect fire at the source — earlier than traditional ceiling-mounted detectors.
  • Sub-second response and on-camera AI verification.
  • Integration with VMS, BMS and fire alarm control panels.
  • Wide coverage with a single sensor — lower install cost for large areas.
