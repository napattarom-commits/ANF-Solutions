HIKVISION Fire Safety — Full Catalogue

All 5 product categories, sold by ANF Solutions. Datasheets available on request.

Conventional

Conventional Fire Alarm System

Reliable, cost-effective conventional fire alarm system with a complete range of detectors, manual call points, and notification appliances.

HF-C108

8-Zone Conventional Control Panel

Compact 8-zone FACP for small to medium buildings; supports detectors, MCPs, and notification devices.

HF-CS1

Conventional Smoke Detector

Photoelectric smoke detector with stable response and built-in fault diagnostics.

HF-CH1

Conventional Heat Detector

Fixed / rate-of-rise heat detector for kitchens, parking lots, and dusty environments.

HF-CMP1

Conventional Manual Call Point

Resettable MCP with key reset, transparent cover, and clear status indication.

HF-CAB1

Conventional Bell

Heavy-duty alarm bell, high decibel output, IP-rated for indoor / sheltered outdoor use.

HF-CSS1

Conventional Sounder Strobe

Combined sounder + strobe notification appliance for clear audible and visual alarm.

Fusion Camera

Heat & Flame Detection Camera

AI-enabled vision-based fire detection cameras combining thermal imaging, dual-infrared flame sensing, and visible-light video for large open spaces.

HF-VH221 / VH243

Heat Detection Camera

Thermal heat detection camera for warehouses, logistics centres, and industrial sites.

HF-VH343

Heat Detection Camera (HD)

Higher-resolution thermal model with extended detection range and dual-spectrum view.

HF-VR343

Dual-IR Flame Detection Camera

Dual infrared flame sensing for early detection in petrochemical, EV, and energy sites.

HF-VR344

Fire Detection Camera

Combined fire / smoke detection camera with AI analytics and rapid alarm response.

HF-VT344

Thermal Fire Detection Camera

Dedicated thermal imaging fire detection camera for hot-spot identification.

HF-VTR344

3-in-1 Flame Detection Camera

Thermal + dual-IR + visible-light fusion camera — flagship of the HIKVISION fire detection line.

LoRa Wireless

Wireless Fire Alarm (LoRa)

Long-range, low-power wireless fire alarm products — ideal for retrofit projects, heritage buildings, and sites where wiring is impractical.

NP-FTG200

LoRa Gateway

Central LoRa gateway connecting wireless detectors to the fire workstation.

NP-FY200

LoRa Photoelectric Smoke Detector

Wireless photoelectric smoke detector with long battery life and supervisory signaling.

NP-FW100

LoRa Heat Detector

Wireless heat detector for environments unsuitable for smoke sensing.

HK-SG-1124

LoRa Horn Strobe

Wireless horn-strobe notification appliance, synchronizable across multiple zones.

HK-TX-1400

Fire Workstation CRT

Graphical fire workstation displaying real-time alarm status across LoRa and conventional zones.

Standalone

Standalone Detectors & Alarms

Self-contained smoke, gas, and CO detectors for residential, hospitality, and small commercial use — including Wi-Fi connected and interlinkable models.

HF-S2

Photoelectric Smoke Alarm

Standalone photoelectric smoke alarm with 10-year battery and audible alarm.

HF-S2E

Eco Photoelectric Smoke Alarm

Economical model retaining full safety performance for residential use.

HF-S3E / S3E-R

Interlink Smoke Detector

Smoke detector with interlink option — one alarm triggers all networked devices.

HF-S3

Wi-Fi Smoke Alarm

Wi-Fi connected smoke alarm with mobile app notification and remote diagnostics.

HF-GM100

CO Alarm

Standalone carbon monoxide alarm — essential for kitchens, boiler rooms, and garages.

HF-GN110 / GP110

Combustible Gas Detector

Natural gas (HF-GN110) and LPG (HF-GP110) leakage detector with built-in audible alarm.

Cable

Shielded Fire Alarm Cable

Certified fire alarm cable for conventional and LoRa systems — shielded construction for noise immunity and signal integrity.

HF-PC01

2C × 18AWG Shielded Fire Alarm Cable

Standard shielded twin-core 18AWG fire alarm cable for detector and notification loops.

HF-PC03

2C × 16AWG Shielded Fire Alarm Cable

Heavier 16AWG version for longer runs and higher current notification appliance circuits.
